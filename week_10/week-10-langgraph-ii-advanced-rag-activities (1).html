    Corte 2 · Week 10 of 16

    Lab & Activities

    Quiz, a live human-in-the-loop RAG graph, a project-design workshop, an individual lab wiring everything into your own project, peer review, and the full Corte 2 delivery rubric.

    How this session runs

    2 hours of theory (content page) + 4 hours of hands-on lab (this page). This is the last content week of Corte 2 — most of the lab time today is deliberately spent turning today's hybrid-search + re-ranking + HITL graph into real progress on your project, since Week 11 is the exam / project delivery week.

    How this counts toward your grade

    Corte 2 = 30% of the course: 25% practical project (delivered Week 11) + 5% in-class activities (cumulative across Weeks 6–11). The rubric for the project is below — read it now, not the night before it's due.

    Activity 1 · Warm-up Concept Quiz

    10 minIndividualFormative

    1. What does interrupt() rely on to make a pause resumable, even much later?

    Correct answer: B. The same checkpointer mechanism from Week 9 is what makes resuming possible.

    2. Why does pure vector search sometimes miss an obviously relevant chunk containing an exact error code?

    Correct answer: A. Hybrid search adds a keyword path specifically to catch these cases.

    3. Why re-rank only the top-k retrieved chunks instead of the whole corpus?

    Correct answer: C. Retrieval trades precision for speed at corpus scale; re-ranking recovers precision on a small candidate set.

    Activity 2 · Instructor-Led Live Coding

    40 minWhole group

    Run agents/week-10-langgraph-ii-advanced-rag/langgraph/app.py: a retrieve → generate → review graph. Ask a question the sample documents answer well (no interrupt), then one it answers poorly (triggers the review interrupt) — approve, edit, and reject it live to see all three resume paths.

    Activity 3 · Group Workshop: Where Should Your Project Pause for a Human?

    30 minGroups of 3–4

    For your Corte 2 project design:

    1. Identify one decision point that should not be fully autonomous (a good interrupt() candidate) and justify why.
    2. Decide what "low confidence" would mean concretely for your system — a numeric threshold, a missing-context flag, or something else.
    3. Decide whether hybrid search is worth the extra complexity for your document set, or whether pure vector search is enough — justify either answer.

    Activity 4 · Individual Lab: Wire It Into Your Project

    90 minIndividualGraded (activities)
    • Add re-ranking to your project's retrieval step (cross-encoder or LLM-based — your choice).
    • Add at least one interrupt() point guarding a consequential or low-confidence action.
    • Test the full resume cycle: trigger the interrupt, resume with an approval and with an edit, confirm both paths work.
    • Commit your progress under week-10/ in your course GitHub repository.
